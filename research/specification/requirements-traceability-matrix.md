Generated by a private generator script from [`business/desk-responsibilities.csv`](../business/desk-responsibilities.csv) (49 rows, R1) and S1's mapping. Machine-readable copy: [`specification/traceability.csv`](traceability.csv). FR/AC ids refer to [`specification/mvp-specification.md`](mvp-specification.md); ADRs are in [`architecture/`](../architecture). The generator is not published; it exited non-zero if a responsibility was unmapped.

**Columns.** `mvp`: IN = served by the MVP; PARTIAL = the text part is served, the rest is OUT (reason given); OUT = not served. `min_mode`: the lowest operating mode (ADR-006) at which the MVP part works: O1 deterministic, O2 with single LLM calls, O3 with embeddings.

## 1. Coverage, stated honestly

- Of 49 responsibilities: **IN 11**, **PARTIAL 13**, **OUT 25**.
- Of the IN/PARTIAL rows, the lowest mode needed: O1 19, O2 5, O3 0. O3 is never the minimum; it improves grouping quality (pair F1 0.78 vs 0.37) where O1 already functions.
- **The video half of the 외신 seat is not reached.** Every footage-watching, ingest, archive, clip-metadata and video feed-fault duty (W3 entirely; R-02, R-07, R-16, R-25) is OUT (ADR-007). By duty count this is about half of the seat (`01_INDUSTRY…` F-A-01).
- **The AP/APTN/Reuters duties** (R-01, R-02, R-12) map to the source connectors of ADR-010: text arrives through the directory watcher (AP Media Agent output: NewsML-G2, NITF; VERIFIED layout in `02_COMMERCIAL…` §8.1) or, later, the `wire_api` connector with the customer's key. The MVP demonstrates this on synthetic fixtures only; real wire content requires the customer's licence and the customer-entered, attested flags (FR-30). APTN footage stays OUT.
- **Every clipping-service duty (W5) is OUT** under D3/Model B; the mechanisms they need (profiles, schedules, review-before-send, spike alerts) exist in the MVP for later reuse.
- **In O1 the Korean summarise/translate duties (R-05, R-06, R-10, R-17) are not performed**: the monitor reads originals.
